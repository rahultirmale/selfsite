How I lead product

02 — Principles

Business outcome before feature

Every roadmap item traces to a number the business cares about: MAU, revenue, GMV or cost to serve. If it doesn't move one of them, it waits.

Research is leverage

A week understanding why users pick the wrong plan saves a quarter building the wrong thing. I put discovery inside delivery instead of treating it as a separate phase.

Technology serves the job

I've shipped LLM agents and gamification engines, and I'd ship a simpler button if it did the job better. The tool follows the problem.

Point of view

05 — Writing
Build vs. buy vs. rent

Closed APIs are perfect for MVPs and terrible for scale

Calling ChatGPT or Claude means renting compute you can't inspect, fine-tune or host, and the bill grows with every user. I hosted an open-weight model myself to understand the real economics.

  • Run locally with Ollama in 10 minutes; use Docker + vLLM on a cloud GPU for production
  • Fine-tuning and prompt engineering solve different problems
  • Owning the stack gives you control of your data and better unit economics
